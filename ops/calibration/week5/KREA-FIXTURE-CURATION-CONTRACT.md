# Week-5 Krea fixture curation contract

**Status:** source selection open; no fixture is approved yet  
**Production impact:** none  
**Purpose:** define what D1/D2 and sealed C1-C4 must prove before any GPU run

Candidate-source research is recorded separately in
`KREA-FIXTURE-CANDIDATE-SOURCES-2026-07-27.md`. Those candidates are inputs to
human review only; referencing them here does not approve a source or split.

## Experimental roles

- **D1:** a subject/identity-shaped concept with 18–24 training pairs and 24
  held-out exact-evaluation rows.
- **D2:** a materially different style/product/domain concept with 36–48
  training pairs and 40 held-out exact-evaluation rows.
- **C1–C4:** four additional concepts selected and privately sealed by the
  independent reviewer before D1/D2 results are unblinded. They are confirmation
  evidence, not additional discovery opportunities. Their public, exact shapes
  are:

  | Role | Concept class | Training pairs | Evaluation rows |
  |---|---|---:|---:|
  | C1 | architectural object | 20 | 6 |
  | C2 | art/print-style series | 45 | 6 |
  | C3 | natural subject | 30 | 8 |
  | C4 | product/design object set | 12 | 5 |

  These are four distinct concept/shape contracts. C1/C2 are not aliases for
  the discovery `small` boundary, and C3/C4 are not aliases for `large`.

The exact fixture identities remain unset; only the four public concept classes
above are known. Filling the identities is an evidence-changing decision that
requires a named source owner and reviewer; a convenient directory of images
is not authorization to use it.

## Admission rules

Every source must have a recorded owner, retrieval URL or immutable local
source, retrieval time, license or owner authorization, and a SHA-256-bound
license/authorization record. Do not admit material whose training/evaluation
rights are unclear. Do not use this week's hidden/publicized tournament
fixtures, the Week-4 Krea calibration concept, or images generated by any arm
under comparison.

Each concept must:

1. use one stable trigger token and one caption policy across every arm;
2. contain the declared number of decodable image/caption pairs;
3. preserve the tournament input and exact-evaluator formats;
4. cover meaningful within-concept variation in pose, composition, lighting,
   scale, and background rather than near-duplicate crops;
5. have no file-byte, normalized-pixel, caption, or perceptual near-duplicate
   overlap between training and evaluation, between D1 and D2, or with C1–C4;
6. be frozen before the first arm is trained and never repaired in place.

The split checker must hash the decoded normalized pixels independently of
filenames and container metadata, bind the exact near-duplicate detector and
threshold, and fail closed on unreadable images, missing captions, duplicate
normalized captions, ambiguous pairings, or an unexpected file.

## Required manifest per concept

The canonical split manifest must bind:

- concept ID, experimental role, trigger token, and caption-policy SHA-256;
- source/rights record and its SHA-256;
- every relative path, split, byte SHA-256, normalized-pixel SHA-256,
  normalized-caption SHA-256, dimensions, and media type;
- the split-tool source SHA-256, decoder/library identities, perceptual-hash
  algorithm and threshold, and the complete cross-split comparison result;
- train/evaluation aggregate digests and the canonical manifest SHA-256;
- named preparer, named independent reviewer, review time, and an external
  approval record that binds the manifest digest.

Paths are descriptive only. GPU tools must consume an exclusively staged,
rehash-verified copy bound to the approved manifest, not mutable source paths.

## Freeze and unblinding order

1. Source owner prepares D1/D2 and their manifests.
2. Independent reviewer checks rights, concept distinction, counts, and
   disjointness, then approves the exact digests.
3. Independent reviewer privately selects and seals C1–C4.
4. The execution plan binds D1/D2 manifests, arm conditions, evaluator/runtime
   identities, timing profiles, runner source, timeouts, and reviewer approval.
5. Only then may a GPU profile or discovery run begin.
6. D1/D2 results freeze finalists and the deterministic checkpoint rule.
7. C1–C4 identities are revealed once; failed confirmation sends the policy
   back to discovery and cannot be relabeled as a win.

The C1–C4 counts above were added after the public commitment exposed the
earlier plan's incorrect small/large generalization. This is a metadata
amendment, recorded in `krea-c1c4-shape-contract-amendment.json`; it does not
reseal or change fixture bytes, manifest digests, identities, custody, or the
published aggregate commitment.

## Claim limit

Passing the split contract prevents leakage and provenance ambiguity. It does
not make two concepts representative of the tournament and does not make any
recipe competitive. Those claims require the predeclared discovery,
confirmation, public-family parity, regret, and boundary gates.
